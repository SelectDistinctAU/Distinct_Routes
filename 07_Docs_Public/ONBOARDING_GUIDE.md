# Quick Start Onboarding Guide — Distinct Routes

Welcome to **Distinct Routes**! In less than 60 seconds, you can plan your first safety-focused ride.

---

### Step 1: Select Your Vehicle
At the top of the **Route Planner** screen, tap the **Selected Vehicle / Mobility Device** box and pick your riding profile (e.g. *Standard Bicycle, E-Bike, Cargo Bike, Wheelchair, or Mobility Scooter*). Speed and hill safety rules will adjust automatically.

---

### Step 2: Set Your Waypoints
* **Start:** Type your starting address or tap the **GPS Target Icon** (`⊕`) inside the field to detect your live location.
* **Via Stops (Optional):** Tap **+ Add Stop Along the Way (Via)** to add intermediate stops or create a loop trail.
* **Destination:** Type your destination address.

---

### Step 3: Calculate Your Safe Route
Tap **Calculate Safety-Focused Route**. Distinct Routes will calculate a path prioritizing quiet backstreets, cycleways, and flat ground, displaying the route polyline on the map.

---

### Step 4: Save or Navigate
* Tap **Save to Favourites** to save the route to your Favourites list.
* Tap **Export GPX / Navigate** to open the route in OsmAnd, Organic Maps, or export to your Garmin bike computer for turn-by-turn voice guidance while riding!

---

### Need Help or Customizing Settings?
Tap the **Settings** tab at the bottom to set your default city (e.g. Perth, Berlin, London), switch between **Light Pine** and **Dark Night** themes, or view our **Privacy Statement**.
