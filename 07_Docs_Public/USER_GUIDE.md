## 1. Getting Started

When you first open Distinct Routes, you will see the **Route Planner** screen:
* **Header Bar:** Shows the Distinct Routes logo and app title.
* **Vehicle Selector:** Positioned at the very top so you can pick your vehicle before searching addresses.
* **Address Input Fields:** Start Address (with live GPS button) and Destination Address.
* **Add Stop Button:** Add intermediate stops along the way.
* **Interactive Map:** Displays Start (Green Play ▶), Via (Yellow Dot ●), and Destination (Red Stop ■) pins with your calculated route polyline.

---

## 2. Selecting Your Vehicle & Mobility Profile

Tap the **Selected Vehicle / Mobility Device** box at the top of the screen to open the vehicle profile dropdown:

| Profile Name | Calculated Speed | Slope & Highway Rules |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Standard Bicycle** | 15 km/h | Standard cycleway and quiet street weighting |
| **City / Utility Bike** | 14 km/h | Comfortable upright riding speed |
| **E-Bike / Pedelec** | 20 km/h | Assisted speed profile |
| **Mountain Bike (MTB)** | 12 km/h | Unpaved path friendly |
| **Cargo Bike** | 10–12 km/h | Heavy load climbing weight penalty |
| **Tandem Bike** | 13 km/h | Extended vehicle turning radius considerations |
| **Wheelchair / Handbike** | 6 km/h | **Mandatory Avoid Steep Hills & Highways** |
| **Mobility Scooter** | 8 km/h | **Mandatory Avoid Steep Hills & Highways** |

## 3. Planning a Route

1. **Enter Start Address:**
   * Type an address (e.g. *"Alexanderplatz, Berlin"* or *"Perth Station"*) and pick a suggestion from the dropdown.
   * Or tap the **GPS Target Icon** (`⊕`) inside the Start field to use your current location.
2. **Enter Destination Address:**
   * Type your destination address.
3. **Calculate Route:**
   * Tap **Calculate Safety-Focused Route** at the bottom. The app will fetch a quiet, safety-weighted path and display the route polyline on the map along with distance, duration, and safety summary badges.

---

## 4. Adding Multi-Stop Trails & Loops

To create a multi-stop trail or loop:
1. Tap **+ Add Stop Along the Way (Via)**.
2. Type an address for **Via Stop #1**.
3. Tap **+ Add Another Stop (Trail/Loop)** to add additional stops (Via 2, Via 3...).
4. Use the **Up/Down arrow icons** (`↑` / `↓`) next to any Via stop to reorder waypoints, or tap the **Close icon (`✕`)** to remove a stop.
5. Use the **Swap Button (`⇅`)** next to the Destination field to reverse your Start and Destination waypoints.

## 5. Customizing Safety & Riding Preferences

Tap the **Safety & Riding Preferences** card to expand safety toggles:
* **Prefer Green & Scenic Routes:** Prioritizes parks, gardens, and river paths.
* **Avoid Unlit Streets (Night Safety):** Bypasses unlit streets after dark.
* **Avoid Cobblestones / Unpaved Surfaces:** Smooth riding for wheelchairs, mobility scooters, and road bikes.
* **Avoid Ferries:** Avoids water ferry crossings.
* **Avoid Traffic Lights:** Bypasses major signalized intersections.
* **Avoid Highways & Avoid Steep Hills:** Enforced automatically for mobility devices.
* **Dog Trailer Mode:** Applies a 1.5x weight penalty for heavy towing.

## 6. Interactive Map Pin Dropping

You can adjust route waypoints visually on the map:
* **Start Pin (Green Play ▶):** Drag the green pin on the map to fine-tune your start location.
* **Via Pin (Yellow Dot ●):** Drag the yellow pin to adjust intermediate stops.
* **Destination Pin (Red Stop ■):** Drag the red pin to fine-tune your destination.
* **Quick Tap:** Long-press anywhere on the map to set the Start pin; single-tap to set the Destination pin.

---

## 7. Saving Favourites & Viewing Ride Journal

* **Save to Favourites:** Tap **Save to Favourites** on the calculated route result card to save the route to your permanent list.
* **Favourites Tab:** Switch to the **Favourites** bottom navigation tab:
  * **[ Favourites ] Tab:** View saved routes with 1-tap **Load into Route Planner** or **Delete**.
  * **[ Ride Journal ] Tab:** Review chronological history of all calculated rides logged locally on device with a **Clear Journal** option.

---

## 8. Exporting GPX & Turn-by-Turn Navigation

Distinct Routes plans the safest route and lets you hand off navigation to your preferred handlebar app or GPS device:
* **Export GPX / Navigate:** Tap **Export GPX / Navigate** to hand off the `.gpx` track file directly to **OsmAnd**, **Organic Maps**, or **Garmin Connect**.
* **Share Route:** Tap **Share Route** to open the Android system share sheet and send route summaries or `.gpx` files to contacts via SMS, WhatsApp, or Email.

---

## 9. Customizing App Appearance & Settings

Switch to the **Settings** bottom navigation tab to customize defaults:
1. **Route & Vehicle Defaults:** Set your default BBBike City (e.g. Perth, Berlin, London) and default Vehicle Profile.
2. **Appearance & Safety Defaults:** Toggle **App Theme Mode** (**Light Pine**, **Dark Night**, or **System Default**), and set persistent default safety toggles.
3. **About & Privacy:** Review the **Why Distinct Routes?** safety mission, tap **View Privacy Statement**, and check version/license information.
