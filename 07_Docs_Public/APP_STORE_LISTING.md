## 2. Full Description (Google Play Store & Web)

**Distinct Routes** is a free, open-source, safety-focused route planner designed specifically for cyclists, cargo bike riders, wheelchair users, and mobility device operators.

Unlike conventional map services that route bikes onto high-speed highways or steep hills just to save a few seconds, Distinct Routes prioritizes **quiet neighborhood backstreets, designated cycleways, flat terrain, and green scenic paths**.

Powered by **BBBike.org** and **OpenStreetMap**, Distinct Routes gives you complete control over your route based on your specific vehicle type and personal safety preferences.

---

### Key Features & Capabilities

* **Safety-First Pathfinding:**
  * Bypasses dangerous multi-lane traffic arterials in favor of low-speed, calm urban streets and dedicated bike paths.
  * Custom safety toggles: **Avoid Traffic Lights**, **Avoid Highways**, **Avoid Steep Hills**, **Prefer Green & Scenic Routes**, **Avoid Unlit Streets (Night Safety)**, **Avoid Cobblestones / Unpaved Surfaces**, and **Avoid Ferries**.

* **Specialized Vehicle & Mobility Device Profiles:**
  * **Standard Bicycle** (15 km/h) & **City / Utility Bike** (14 km/h)
  * **E-Bike / Pedelec** (20 km/h speed profile)
  * **Mountain Bike (MTB)** & **Trekking Bike**
  * **Cargo Bike** & **Tandem Bike**
  * **Dog Trailer Mode:** Enforces an automatic 1.5x weight/steep hill climbing penalty for heavy towing.
  * **Wheelchair & Handbike:** 6 km/h max speed calculation with mandatory steep hill and highway avoidance rules.
  * **Mobility Scooter:** 8 km/h speed profile with mandatory steep hill and highway avoidance rules.

* **Multi-Stop Trail & Loop Creation:**
  * Add multiple intermediate Via stops (Via 1, Via 2, Via 3...) to design custom loop trails, scenic weekend rides, or multi-destination errands.
  * Easy drag-and-drop pin placement on the interactive map, plus Up/Down reorder and Start/Destination swap controls.

* **1-Tap GPX Export & Turn-by-Turn Navigation:**
  * Export standard `.gpx` files or hand off your route directly to turn-by-turn handlebar navigation apps (OsmAnd, Organic Maps) or bicycle GPS computers (Garmin, Wahoo, Bryton).
  * Native Android Share Sheet support to share routes with contacts via SMS, WhatsApp, or Email.

* **On-Device Ride Journal & Favourites:**
  * Save favorite routes for 1-tap reloading into the planner.
  * On-device Ride Journal automatically logs calculated routes locally so you can review past rides anytime.

* **Global City Coverage:**
  * Live dynamic city list fetching from BBBike.org, supporting Perth (WA), Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Canberra, Berlin, London, Paris, Rome, Madrid, Zurich, Vienna, New York, Tokyo, and 230+ global regions.

* **Native Commonwealth & Multi-Language Support:**
  * Native English localizations for **United Kingdom (`en-GB`)**, **Australia (`en-AU`)**, and **New Zealand (`en-NZ`)** with correct "Favourite" spellings.
  * Complete translations in **German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, and Mandarin Chinese**.

* **100% Private & Open Source (GPLv3):**
  * **Zero User Accounts:** No registration or login required.
  * **Zero Telemetry & Zero Ads:** All saved routes, preferences, and ride history remain 100% on your device.
