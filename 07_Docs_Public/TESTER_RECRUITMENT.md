## What is Distinct Routes?

Distinct Routes is a free, open-source Android application that plans safety-focused routes prioritized for **calm backstreets, quiet cycleways, flat terrain, and greenways** rather than high-speed motorways.

It features specialized profiles for:
* **Cyclists & E-Bikers:** Avoid traffic lights, steep hills, cobblestones, and unlit streets.
* **Cargo Bikes & Dog Trailers:** Automatic weight climbing penalties.
* **Wheelchairs & Mobility Scooters:** Automatic steep hill and highway avoidance rules with realistic 6–8 km/h travel times.
* **Trail & Loop Riders:** Unlimited multi-stop waypoints for custom recreational loops.
