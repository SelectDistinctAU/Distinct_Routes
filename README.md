## ABOUT

Distinct Routes is a free, open-source Android application that plans safety-focused routes prioritized for **calm backstreets, quiet cycleways, flat terrain, and greenways** rather than high-speed motorways.

**Key features:**
- **Dynamic City Selection:** Fetch live BBBike cities worldwide (Perth, Berlin, London, Sydney, Paris, and 230+ regions)
- **Specialized Vehicle Profiles:** Standard Bicycles, E-Bikes, Cargo Bikes (with Dog Trailer weight penalties), Tandems, **Wheelchairs / Handbikes** (6 km/h max, hill rules), and **Mobility Scooters** (8 km/h max)
- **Safety Preferences:** Green/scenic routes, unlit street avoidances, cobblestone/surface avoidances, ferry avoidances, traffic lights, highways, and steep hills
- **Multi-Stop Trail Creation:** Unlimited intermediate Via waypoints with Up/Down reordering and Start/Destination swap
- **1-Tap Navigation Handoff & Share:** Export GPX directly to OsmAnd, Organic Maps, Garmin, or share via Android system share sheet
- **On-Device Ride Journal & Favourites:** Persistent local favourites and automatic ride history logging (zero cloud tracking)
- **Material 3 Emerald/Pine Theme System:** Dual brand color palettes (Metallic Blue vs Emerald Pine) in Light and Dark Night modes
- **Multi-Language Support:** Native UK (`en-GB`), AU (`en-AU`), and NZ (`en-NZ`) Commonwealth "Favourite" spellings + German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, and Mandarin Chinese

## DISCLAIMER

**This app does NOT guarantee route safety.** We provide routing data, not safety guarantees. Cyclists and mobility device operators remain responsible for assessing local conditions before riding. This app surfaces routing data from BBBike — refer to BBBike's documentation for more information.
